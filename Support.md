# PrettyMe Support

Need help with PrettyMe? We’re happy to help.

## Contact Support

For questions, technical issues, subscription concerns, or feedback, contact us at:

**[prettymeapp2@gmail.com](mailto:prettymeapp2@gmail.com)**

When contacting support, please include:

* A brief description of the issue
* Your device model and iOS version, if relevant
* Your **PrettyMe User ID**, which can be found in the app under **Settings**

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your support request.

## Subscriptions

PrettyMe Pro subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID.

To view, change, or cancel a subscription:

**iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → PrettyMe**

Subscription billing and refunds are handled through Apple.

## Privacy

For information about how PrettyMe handles your data and photos, please review our **Privacy Policy**.
