# PrettyMe Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 10, 2026**

PrettyMe is operated by **LooksLab** (“PrettyMe,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), located at 490 Post St, Ste 500 PMB 2458, San Francisco, CA 94102.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and process when you use the PrettyMe app, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices available to you.

You can contact us at **[prettymeapp2@gmail.com](mailto:prettymeapp2@gmail.com)**.

## The short version

* You do not need to provide your name, email address, or phone number, and you do not create a traditional user profile.
* We create an anonymous app identifier so PrettyMe can function and associate your usage, history, and subscription status with your app session.
* Photos you select for editing or analysis are uploaded to our cloud infrastructure and may be sent to **Google Nano Banana 2** or **OpenAI ChatGPT Images 2.0** to perform the edit or analysis you request.
* Uploaded photos and generated results are retained for up to 1 year unless deleted earlier at your request.
* We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
* **We do not use your photos or generated images to train AI models.**
* Face detection used to validate a photo is performed on your device. We do not send the resulting face-detection measurements or landmarks to our servers.
* You may request deletion of your PrettyMe data at any time by contacting us at **[prettymeapp2@gmail.com](mailto:prettymeapp2@gmail.com)**.
* PrettyMe is intended only for users who are **18 years of age or older**.

## Information we collect

### Photos you select

When you select a photo to edit or analyze, PrettyMe accesses the specific photo you choose and uploads it to our cloud infrastructure.

Depending on the feature you use, the selected photo may then be transmitted to one of our third-party AI providers:

* **Google Nano Banana 2**
* **OpenAI ChatGPT Images 2.0**

These services process the image and instructions necessary to perform the AI edit or analysis you requested.

Photos may contain personal information, including facial imagery and information about other people or objects appearing in the image.

We use selected photos only to provide, operate, secure, and support the PrettyMe features you request.

**PrettyMe does not use your photos to train AI models. We do not sell your photos, use them for advertising, or share them with advertisers or data brokers.**

Before transmitting a selected photo to a third-party AI provider, PrettyMe provides an in-app disclosure explaining that the image will be sent to an external AI service and requests your permission where required.

### Generated images and analyses

Images, edits, recommendations, and analyses generated through PrettyMe may be stored on our servers for up to 1 year so they can appear in your PrettyMe history.

Generated content may also be cached locally on your device to improve loading performance.

**PrettyMe does not use generated images or analyses to train AI models.**

### Anonymous app identifier

PrettyMe uses Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous user identifier.

This identifier allows us to associate information such as your edit history, usage limits, processing jobs, and subscription entitlement with your PrettyMe session without requiring you to provide a name, email address, password, or phone number.

We do not intentionally associate this identifier with your real-world identity.

Your anonymous PrettyMe identifier can be found in the **Settings** section of the app.

If your anonymous authentication state is lost or replaced, including in some cases after deleting or reinstalling the app, you may lose access to history associated with the previous identifier.

### Subscription and purchase information

If you purchase a PrettyMe subscription, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment-card information.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription purchases and entitlements.

RevenueCat may receive information about your App Store purchase, including:

* The subscription product purchased
* Subscription status
* Renewal status
* Purchase and expiration information
* Your anonymous PrettyMe identifier

This allows PrettyMe to determine whether premium features should be available to you.

We may also record subscription-related events, such as when a paywall is displayed, selected, dismissed, or followed by a purchase, to understand subscription performance and improve the service.

### App integrity information

We use Firebase App Check with Apple's app-integrity technologies, including App Attest or DeviceCheck where applicable, to help verify that requests originate from a legitimate installation of PrettyMe.

This helps us prevent automated abuse, fraud, unauthorized API use, and manipulation of usage limits.

This process may involve app or device attestation information, integrity tokens, and related technical information used for security purposes.

### Technical and diagnostic information

When PrettyMe communicates with our servers, we may receive technical information such as:

* IP address
* Request timestamps
* App version
* Operating-system information
* Request and processing-job information
* Error codes
* Diagnostic information
* Performance information
* Security and abuse-prevention information

We use this information to operate PrettyMe, troubleshoot problems, monitor performance, prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, and protect the service.

## On-device processing

Some PrettyMe processing occurs directly on your device.

### Face detection

PrettyMe uses Google ML Kit to check whether a selected image contains a suitable face before it is uploaded for processing.

This may include checking:

* Whether a face is present
* The number of detected faces
* Face position
* Face orientation
* Facial landmarks or related measurements used to determine whether the photo is suitable for a requested feature

These face-detection results are processed locally by PrettyMe and are not transmitted by PrettyMe to our servers.

Google ML Kit may process limited SDK, application, device, performance, or diagnostic information according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

### Preferences and cached data

PrettyMe may store information locally on your device, including:

* Display preferences
* App settings
* Cached copies of generated images
* Authentication information required to maintain your anonymous session

This local storage helps PrettyMe load more quickly and preserve your app experience.

Deleting PrettyMe from your device removes locally stored PrettyMe data, subject to normal operating-system behavior, but does not automatically delete information already stored on our servers.

## Access to your photo library

When you choose a photo, PrettyMe accesses only the photo or photos you specifically select through the applicable system photo-selection interface or permissions you provide.

We do not intentionally browse, copy, or collect your entire photo library.

If you choose to save a generated image, PrettyMe may request permission to add that specific image to your photo library.

PrettyMe may display a local notification confirming that an image has been saved. Local notifications are generated on your device.

PrettyMe does not currently use remote push notifications or collect push-notification tokens.

## Facial imagery and face data

Many PrettyMe features operate on photographs containing faces. We therefore process facial imagery when necessary to provide the editing or analysis feature you request.

We treat facial imagery as sensitive information and limit its use to providing, operating, securing, and supporting PrettyMe.

### On-device face detection

Face detection used to determine whether a photo is suitable for processing occurs on your device.

Face-detection measurements generated during this validation process are not transmitted by PrettyMe to our servers.

### Facial imagery sent for AI processing

When you request an AI-powered edit or analysis, the selected image itself may be transmitted to our cloud infrastructure and to:

* **Google Nano Banana 2**, or
* **OpenAI ChatGPT Images 2.0**

The image is sent only as necessary to perform the feature you requested.

### What we do not do with face data

PrettyMe does not:

* Use facial imagery to determine your identity
* Create or store a faceprint designed to uniquely identify you
* Create a persistent biometric facial template for recognition purposes
* Use facial imagery for surveillance
* Use facial imagery for advertising
* Sell facial imagery
* Share facial imagery with advertisers or data brokers
* Use facial imagery to train AI models

PrettyMe's facial editing and analysis features are designed to modify or evaluate the appearance shown in the image you choose, not to identify who the person is.

PrettyMe uses third-party generative AI services to perform image editing and analysis.

Depending on the requested feature, PrettyMe may use:

### Google Nano Banana 2

PrettyMe may transmit your selected image and the instructions necessary to perform the requested edit or analysis to Google's Nano Banana 2 image-processing service.

### OpenAI ChatGPT Images 2.0

PrettyMe may transmit your selected image and the instructions necessary to perform the requested edit or analysis to OpenAI's ChatGPT Images 2.0 service.

### Our use of AI data

PrettyMe uses these AI providers solely to provide the editing and analysis features you request.

**PrettyMe does not use your submitted photos, generated images, or analysis results to train AI models.**

Our third-party providers may process or temporarily retain information as necessary to provide their services, maintain security, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We do not authorize our AI providers to use your information for advertising on behalf of PrettyMe.
